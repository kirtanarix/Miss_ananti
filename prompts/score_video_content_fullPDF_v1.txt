Judge SA, Goals, Purpose, Clarity and Voice, once each, from the whole transcript and the
prompt-to-parameter map. Use what was said; do not infer eye contact, gestures, confidence,
hesitation, reading or any other delivery trait. For Clarity, judge the meaning, relevance
and logical structure of the spoken content as represented in the transcript. Grammar,
accent, English fluency, vocabulary sophistication, speaking speed, answer length and
production quality do not earn or lose marks. Name and city are context only. Common goals
can score highly when personal reasons and direction support them. Do not require an extended
introduction when the introduction prompt requests only name and city.

For each parameter choose one INTEGER level 0 through 8. Use the PDF's 0/2/4/6/8 anchors;
an odd level requires an explanation of what is met in the lower and higher neighboring
anchors. Do not add requirements beyond a descriptor. Explain how the best evidence supports
the selected anchor and what, if anything, prevents the next higher anchor. Level 8 has no
next anchor. Use all relevant evidence across the transcript; do not average prompt scores.

For each nonzero content parameter, return both (a) one or more short evidence phrases and
(b) the exact complete transcript sentence or short utterance containing each phrase. Copy
the excerpt exactly, preserving language, spelling, punctuation and word order. Each excerpt

absence in absence_observation. Never treat an unclear transcript or unsupported language as
